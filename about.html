About

Aadi Patel

I’m a fourth-year physics student at the University of Lethbridge, drawn to the questions that sit where astronomy, physics and space meet.

My current research is in radio astronomy. Working with Dr. Alex Tetarenko, I calibrate and image ALMA observations of black hole X-ray binaries to understand how these systems accrete matter and launch outflows. Before that I spent time in the Theoretical Physics Group with Dr. Saurya Das studying black hole dynamics, general relativity and neutrino phenomenology, worked on quantum algorithms with Dr. Daya Gaur, and built a computer-vision pipeline for neuroscience research with Dr. Hardeep Ryait.

I’m also interested in medical physics, a direction rooted in four years volunteering with Alberta Health Services at Chinook Regional Hospital. Outside of research I teach as a Calculus I teaching assistant and tutor, founded the Physics, Astronomy, and Mathematics Association, and spend clear nights observing with the Lethbridge Astronomy Society.
